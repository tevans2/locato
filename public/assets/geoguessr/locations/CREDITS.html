Places worth discovering.

Our maps use public panorama metadata from these projects. Street View imagery is provided by Google.

  • G³: Geolocation via Guidebook Grounding
    Grace Luo, Giscard Biamby, Trevor Darrell, Daniel Fried and Anna Rohrbach · Apache 2.0
  • WanderBench: Learning to Wander
    Yushuo Zheng, Huiyu Duan, Zicheng Zhang, Xiaohong Liu and Xiongkuo Min · CC BY 4.0
  • StreetView360AtoZ / StreetView360X
    Everett Shen · MIT

Locato combines and deduplicates these references, groups them by country and converts coordinates and headings into our map format. Ambiguous country assignments are excluded. Google checks availability and supplies the starting coordinates used for scoring when you play.
