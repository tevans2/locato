# Locato caption & tag bank

Every video from `npm run new` already writes `videos/<name>.caption.txt`. Use this bank to swap in a fresher line
so a run of posts doesn't repeat itself.

## Rules (from the research report)

- **Short.** One line, a challenge or a brag prompt. The video does the work.
- **3–4 hashtags**, accurate to the content. Platforms rank by watching and interaction, not by hashtags.
- **Ask for the score.** Comments drive reach: every caption ends in something people can answer.
- **No made-up stats** ("95% fail this"). Only claim what's true. A real Locato stat ("Only 23% of players get this one") is fine once we have the data.
- **Links:** caption links aren't clickable on TikTok or Instagram, so say "link in bio". On YouTube, put the full link in the description.

## Captions by format

`{n}` = number of rounds. Pick one, or mix a hook with an ending.

**Flags**
- {n}/{n} = geography legend 🌍 Drop your score 👇
- Starts easy. Ends with a boss flag. How far did you get?
- Flag check: what's your score out of {n}?
- Number 5 is where most people stop. Did you?
- Name all {n} without pausing. Go.

**Country outlines**
- Just the shape. Name the country 🗺️
- Outlines get hard fast. Score below 👇
- If you get the boss shape, you're a map person.
- Can you name a country by its silhouette?

**Capitals / capital-of**
- Capital cities, easy to brutal. Your score?
- Careful: one of these isn't the city you think 👀
- Name the country from its capital. How many?
- Capitals trip everyone up. Comment your score 👇

**Country codes**
- What country is "CH"? It's not China 👀
- 2 letters, 1 country. How many can you decode?
- Country codes that make no sense. Your score?

**Flag colours**
- Name the flag before the last colour appears 🎨
- One colour at a time. When did you know?
- Guess it at the first colour and you're elite. Score?

**Which one is it? (look-alikes)**
- They look almost the same. Which one is it?
- The look-alike flags that fool everyone. Your score?
- Chad or Romania? Ireland or Ivory Coast? Prove it 👇
- Same colours, different country. Did you spot them all?

**Spot the country**
- The map lights up. Name it 📍
- Can you name a country by where it sits on the map?
- Small countries, big problems. Score?

**Click the country / MapTap**
- Point at your screen: where is it? 📍
- Where's {place}? Be honest about where you pointed.
- Pin it before the timer. How close were you?

**Overland**
- Get from A to B by land. Fewest countries wins 🚗
- How many countries between them? Comment your route 👇
- Some of these routes are… surprising. Agree?
- Land borders only. Did you find the shortest way?

**Worldsplit**
- One line. Which side has more people? 🌏
- Population split: trust your gut or do the maths?
- The answer to #3 surprised us. You?

**Every mode (mix)**
- One round from every Locato mode. Which was hardest?
- Flags, shapes, capitals, maps… can you do them all?

**One Life**
- Miss one and you're out 💀 Where did you get eliminated?
- One life. {n} flags. How far did you make it?
- Survived to the boss? Prove it.

**Daily warm-up**
- Your geography warm-up for {date} 🌅 Today's Daily: {theme}
- Warm up here, then play today's Daily (link in bio)

## Closing lines (add one when the caption is very short)

- Got {n}/{n}? Prove it → link in bio
- Think that was easy? The full game is free → link in bio
- Comment your score and I'll make the next one harder
- Tag someone who'd get them all

## Hashtags

Use 3–4. Core + one format tag (+ one platform tag on YouTube).

| Set | Tags |
|---|---|
| Core (always pick 1–2) | `#geography` `#geographyquiz` `#quiz` |
| Flags, colours, look-alikes | `#flags` `#flagquiz` `#vexillology` |
| Outlines, spot, find, MapTap | `#maps` `#mapquiz` `#worldmap` |
| Capitals, codes | `#capitals` `#countries` `#trivia` |
| Overland, Worldsplit | `#maps` `#puzzle` `#population` |
| Learning angle (sparingly) | `#learnontiktok` `#didyouknow` `#education` |
| YouTube Shorts | `#shorts` plus 2 of the above |

## Pinned comment (post it yourself, then pin it)

- Drop your score: __/{n} 👇
- Which one got you? 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5?
- Hardest one should be tomorrow's boss: name it 👇
- (One Life) Where did you get eliminated? 💀

## Replies (keep the thread going)

- "5/5 easy" → Tomorrow's boss is harder 😏 Full game's in the bio if you want to prove it.
- "That's wrong" (Overland/borders) → Locato counts every land border, including small ones like Ceuta (Spain–Morocco). Would you count it?
- "Do [country/continent] next" → Reply with a video. The next video comes from the comments.
- Wrong guess shared → Same here the first time 😅 Did you get the boss?
